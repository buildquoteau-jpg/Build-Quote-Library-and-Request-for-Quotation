# BQ FibreLine Weatherboard Plank
Category: Cladding / Fibre cement horizontal lap weatherboard
Product code: BQ-FL-WBP
Manufacturer: BQ FibreLine

BQ FibreLine Weatherboard Plank is a pre-primed fibre cement horizontal lap cladding plank for exterior wall applications. Features a smooth face with a fine texture for a contemporary painted finish. Ships in a range of face widths and lengths. Fix with BQ FibreLine FC Nails and approved construction adhesive at all framing members.
