# BQ FibreLine Soffit Sheet
Category: Soffit & Eaves / Fibre cement soffit and eave lining sheet
Product code: BQ-FL-SOF
Manufacturer: BQ FibreLine

BQ FibreLine Soffit Sheet is a pre-primed fibre cement sheet designed for lining the underside of eaves, soffits, carport ceilings and covered outdoor areas. Available in smooth and micro-perforated finishes for passive ventilation. Lightweight and resistant to moisture, insects and rot.
