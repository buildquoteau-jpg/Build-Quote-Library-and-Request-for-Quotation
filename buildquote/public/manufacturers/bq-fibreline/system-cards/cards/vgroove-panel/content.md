# BQ FibreLine VGroove Panel
Category: Cladding / Fibre cement vertically grooved cladding panel
Product code: BQ-FL-VGP
Manufacturer: BQ FibreLine

BQ FibreLine VGroove Panel is a pre-primed fibre cement cladding sheet with machined vertical V-grooves at regular intervals. Produces a modern linear vertical appearance when installed. Available in 133mm and 200mm groove spacings. Fix vertically to horizontal batten framing.
