# BQ InForm VJ Lining
Category: Interior Linings / Tongue-and-groove VJ interior lining sheet
Product code: BQ-IN-VJ
Manufacturer: BQ InForm

BQ InForm VJ Lining is an Australian-made tongue-and-groove MDF lining sheet with a routed V-joint profile. Designed for interior wall and ceiling lining in residential construction. Available primed or in factory-applied colours. Suitable for nail, screw or adhesive fixing to timber or steel framing. Complete the installation with matching InForm cornice, window trim, door trim and joining strips.

## Specifications
- Australian made: yes
- Moisture resistant: yes
