# BQ TimberLock Glulam Hardwood Pergola
Category: Pergolas & Outdoor Structures / Flat-pack Australian hardwood glulam pergola frame
Product code: BQ-TL-GLULAM
Manufacturer: BQ TimberLock

BQ TimberLock Glulam is a flat-pack architectural pergola frame system manufactured from Australian hardwood glulam - H3 treated, finger-jointed and laminated for strength and stability. Sourced from certified Australian plantation and native hardwood mills. Each kit arrives pre-drilled with all members labelled. Proprietary stainless steel fixings, post bases and rafter saddles are included. Available in 3m, 4m and 6m bay widths at 3m rafter span.
