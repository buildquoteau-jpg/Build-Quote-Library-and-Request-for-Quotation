# BQ CladMax Ultra Light Garage Door Cladding
Category: Cladding / Composite garage roller door cladding panel
Product code: BQ-CLADMAX-UL
Manufacturer: BQ Compform

BQ CladMax Ultra Light is a lightweight co-extruded composite cladding system engineered specifically for application to residential garage roller door panels. The low-density timber fibre core and thin-wall polymer cap layer deliver a timber-look finish while remaining within the weight tolerances of standard garage door motors. Available in Standard Slat, Wide Slat and Vented Slat profiles with a dedicated door-frame clip fixing system. Suitable for new and retrofit garage door installations.

## Specifications
- Australian made: yes

- Moisture resistant: yes
