# BQ ScreenMax Composite Privacy Screening
Category: Screening & Fencing / Composite privacy batten screen panel
Product code: BQ-SCREENMAX-COMPOSITE
Manufacturer: BQ Compform

BQ ScreenMax is a co-extruded composite batten screening system with a UV-stable polymer cap layer and timber fibre core. Designed for residential privacy screens, feature fences, carport infill panels and outdoor room dividers. Available in Narrow Batten, Wide Batten and Slatted Panel profiles across a range of contemporary architectural colours.
