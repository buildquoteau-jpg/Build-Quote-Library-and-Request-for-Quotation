# BQ AquaShield External Membrane
Category: Waterproofing / Sheet membrane external waterproofing system
Product code: BQ-AQ-EXT
Manufacturer: BQ AquaShield Waterproofing

BQ AquaShield External Membrane is a torched-on SBS modified bitumen sheet membrane system for external waterproofing of balconies, podiums, planter boxes and below-slab applications. Compliant with AS 4654.2 for external waterproofing. Installed with AquaShield Primer and detail tape at laps and penetrations.
