# BQ AquaShield Wet Area Membrane
Category: Waterproofing / Liquid-applied wet area waterproofing membrane
Product code: BQ-AQ-WAM
Manufacturer: BQ AquaShield Waterproofing

BQ AquaShield Wet Area Membrane is a single-component, liquid-applied polyurethane waterproofing membrane for internal wet areas including bathrooms, ensuites, laundries and shower recesses. Compliant with AS 3740 for internal wet area waterproofing. Applied in two coats over AquaShield Primer with fabric reinforcement at junctions.
