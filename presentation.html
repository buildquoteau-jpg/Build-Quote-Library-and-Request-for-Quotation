What BuildQuote is not

BuildQuote does not hold pricing.

BuildQuote does not hold stock counts.

BuildQuote does not interfere with trusted builder / supplier relationships.

BuildQuote provides suppliers with a clear and detailed bill of materials Request For Quotation —

Simple.

Let's take a look at some of the ways BuildQuote assists…

A simple admin portal.

Links the supplier to thousands of manufacturer products — all in one place.

Listed in the Supplier Directory.

Your business is listed on BuildQuote's customer-facing Supplier Directory — linking you with local trades and contractors who are ready to quote.

One line of code. Infinite products.

Select from thousands of manufactured products to reflect the unique range you stock. Insert one line of code into your own website…

…your website becomes a product library.

Complete with product info, specs, part numbers and links to manufacturer install guides — all on your own site.
