BuildQuote Data Studio

Building system specification intelligence — Explainer: Manufacturer product data to verified system cards funnel.

The Funnel — Step by Step

Step 1

Manufacturer Login

A manufacturer logs into the Data Studio using their account credentials. This portal is a private, controlled space used to manage, update and verify manufacturers own product data.

↓
Step 2

Uploading Product Data

The manufacturer uploads their product information. This can come in several formats:

  • CSV or spreadsheet files — structured data from their own systems
  • PDF documents — datasheets, catalogues, specification documents
  • Other file types — Word documents, images, and more

There's no single required format. The Data Studio is designed to accept whatever the manufacturer already has on hand and make sense of it.

↓
Step 3

Sorting Into Profiles, Variants & Components

Once the data is uploaded, it gets organised into a structured hierarchy that BuildQuote understands:

  • System The broad product family or range (e.g. "Wall Framing System")
  • Profile A specific product line within that system (e.g. "75mm Steel Stud")
  • Variant Variations on that profile — lengths, gauges, colours, finishes
  • Component Individual orderable items, each with a SKU, unit of measure, and specification details

This sorting step turns raw, often messy source data into a clean, consistent product structure that works across the platform.

↓
Step 4

Human Verification

Before publishing, a BuildQuote team member and a manufacturer's representative review the organised data in the Data Studio interface.

This check covers:

  • Are products sorted correctly — right system, right profile, right variant?
  • Are SKUs, unit of measure, and descriptions accurate?
  • Are there any duplicates, gaps, or errors to clean up?

The reviewer can make corrections, flag items for follow-up, or request the manufacturer provide missing information. Nothing moves forward until this check is signed off.

This verification step is the quality gate. It's what keeps the data on the builder-facing side clean and trustworthy.

↓
Step 5

Published to RFQ

Once verified and approved, the product data is published to the live BuildQuote platform.

From here, the components appear as polished, structured system cards that builders can browse, select, and include directly in their Requests for Quotation — sent directly to the builders preferred local supplier.

The data stays owned by the manufacturer, but it now lives inside a format that works for the entire builder workflow — accurate, consistent, and ready to quote from.
